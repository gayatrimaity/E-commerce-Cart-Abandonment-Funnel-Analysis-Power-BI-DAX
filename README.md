# E-Commerce Cart Abandonment & Conversion Funnel Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyses user behaviour across the e-commerce shopping journey to understand why customers abandon their carts, identify critical drop-off points, and highlight opportunities to improve conversion rates.

Using Power BI and DAX, the analysis focuses on cart abandonment patterns, device-based behaviour, and friction points in the conversion funnel.

## 2. Problem Statement
A significant number of users add products to their carts but do not complete their purchases.

The objective of this project is to:
- Understand where users drop off during the purchase journey.
- Identify factors contributing to cart abandonment.
- Compare abandonment behaviour across devices.
- Recommend improvements to reduce friction and support better conversion.

## 3. Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI:** Dashboard development and data visualisation.
- **DAX:** Measures and calculations for analysing key metrics.

## 4. Key Insights

### Mobile Users Show Higher Cart Abandonment
A higher cart abandonment rate was observed among mobile users, suggesting potential usability or user-experience limitations on smaller devices.

### Critical Drop-Off Between Cart and Checkout
Approximately **4,000 users dropped off between the cart and checkout stages**, highlighting a significant friction point in the purchase funnel.

## 5. Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following improvements were proposed:

- **Improve Mobile Experience:** Optimise the mobile shopping interface, navigation, and usability.
- **Simplify Checkout:** Reduce unnecessary steps and make the checkout journey easier to complete.
- **Improve Pricing Transparency:** Display delivery charges, additional fees, and final costs clearly to reduce unexpected surprises during checkout.

## 6. Business Value
The analysis helps identify where potential customers are being lost during the shopping journey and translates those findings into actionable recommendations.

It demonstrates how business data can be used to understand customer behaviour, identify conversion barriers, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 7. Project Deliverables
- Interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Conversion funnel analysis.
- Device-based cart abandonment insights.
- Business recommendations to improve the shopping experience.
